Welcome to the Coffee Guide

Coffee is one of the most popular beverages in the world. Millions of people enjoy it every day because of its rich aroma and energizing effect. 1st October is the international Coffee day.

This website introduces coffee beans, brewing methods, popular drinks and coffee culture. It is designed especially for beginners.
